Privacy Policy

Mhesh Daniel Maina (Damanfit) — Tigoni Ward 2027 Campaign Page

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Mhesh Daniel Maina (Damanfit) campaign Facebook Page ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you interact with our page, our posts, and our associated campaign communications.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with our campaign Facebook Page, Meta (Facebook) may share with us limited information about your engagement, including:

  • Page likes, follows, and reactions on our posts
  • Comments you leave on our posts and our replies to them
  • Messages you send to us through Messenger
  • Public profile information you have chosen to share on Facebook (name, profile picture, location if public)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we receive to:

  • Respond to questions, comments, and messages you send us
  • Share campaign updates, events, calls to action, and volunteer opportunities
  • Measure how our content performs so we can improve our messaging
  • Build a community of supporters in the Ngecha-Tigoni ward and beyond

We do not use this information for commercial advertising, and we do not sell your data to third parties.

3. Sharing of Your Information

We do not share your personal information with any third party except:

  • Volunteer coordinators and campaign staff who have signed confidentiality agreements and need the information to organize campaign activities
  • Law enforcement or regulatory authorities if legally required to do so
  • Service providers who help us run the campaign (for example, an email or SMS tool) under strict confidentiality obligations

4. Your Rights and Choices

You can control how we (and Meta) interact with you at any time:

  • Unlike or unfollow our Page to stop seeing our posts in your feed
  • Hide, delete, or report any comment you have left on our Page
  • Block our Page to prevent all future contact
  • Adjust your ad preferences in Facebook's Ad Settings to limit how your data is used for advertising
  • Request a copy of any personal data we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by contacting us using the details below

5. Data Retention

We retain comments and messages for as long as the campaign is active or as needed for record-keeping, after which they are deleted or anonymized. You may request earlier deletion by contacting us.

6. Data Deletion

If you have interacted with our Page and wish to have your data (such as a comment or Messenger thread) deleted, you may:

  1. Delete the comment yourself from the Page, or
  2. Email us at privacy@damanfit.co.ke with a link to the content you want removed, and we will action it within 14 days

For data Meta holds about you (your account, ad preferences, off-Facebook activity), please use Meta's own privacy tools at facebook.com/privacy.

7. Children

Our Page is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has interacted with us, please contact us so we can remove the data.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision. Material changes will be announced via a Page post.

9. Contact Us

For any questions about this Privacy Policy, your data, or your rights, contact:

  • Email: privacy@damanfit.co.ke
  • Page: MHESH Daniel Maina on Facebook
  • Campaign manager: Daniel Maina Wangui
